# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-10-23*
*Status: V1 confirmed for use; the owner has verified the proof points listed below. Audience and differentiation statements remain hypotheses where marked.*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Waggies provides pet boarding, veterinary care, and request-based pet relocation coordination in Abuja.

**What it does:** Waggies offers pet boarding for dogs and cats, veterinary services, and import/export coordination. Relocation coordination includes permits, veterinary and health documents, customs clearance, airport pickup for imports, and crate arrangements, airline booking, and transport to the departure airport for exports. Waggies does not offer door-to-door delivery. Route feasibility, provider availability, scope, and costs are confirmed in a custom quote. A request is not an instant booking.

**Product category:** Local pet care, veterinary services, boarding, and pet travel coordination.

**Product type:** Local service business.

**Business model:** Service fees for boarding and veterinary care; custom quotes for relocation. Current service pricing is owned by the application configuration.

## Target Audience

**Target customers:** Dog and cat owners in Abuja, including owners planning an import or export move.

**Decision-makers:** The pet owner or the person arranging care and travel for the pet.

**Primary use case:** Find and request pet care or relocation support through one Abuja-based team.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Arrange boarding for a dog or cat.
- Request veterinary care for a dog or cat.
- Understand and coordinate the steps for bringing a pet to Nigeria or exporting one from Nigeria.

**Use cases:** Boarding and veterinary requests in Abuja; route-specific import/export coordination; relocation planning with the site's existing checklist and guides.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Pet owners need to arrange care or travel while understanding availability, requirements, costs, and what happens next.

**Why alternatives may feel difficult:** A relocation can involve several agencies and providers; a boarding or veterinary request also depends on availability and the pet's needs. This is a hypothesis based on the service journey, not customer interview evidence.

**What it costs them:** Time spent understanding requirements and coordinating care or providers. No quantified cost is known.

**Emotional tension:** Owners may worry about their pet's care, travel requirements, timing, and handoffs. This is an audience hypothesis that needs customer-language validation.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Abuja pet-care providers advertise overlapping veterinary, boarding, and pet-travel services. BlissPets, for example, publicly lists veterinary, boarding, and export-permit services in Abuja ([BlissPets](https://blisspetssuperstores.com/about/)). Doggies Dreamland publicly describes a boarding facility ([Doggies Dreamland](https://doggiesdreamland.org/our-boarding-facility/)). These pages establish advertised overlap, not comparative quality or current availability.

**Secondary:** Owners may arrange boarding and veterinary care separately, or coordinate travel requirements directly with agencies, airlines, veterinarians, and airport providers. This is an alternative workflow, not a verified competitor weakness.

**Indirect:** Family, friends, or informal pet sitters may help with care. Whether this is a meaningful alternative for Waggies customers is unknown.

## Differentiation

**Current positioning:** The site presents Waggies as an Abuja pet-care team offering boarding, veterinary care, and pet-travel coordination through a reviewed request process.

**Potential differentiator:** The existing request flow can bring service details and next steps into one conversation. This is a positioning hypothesis; customer research has not established why customers choose Waggies over alternatives.

**Why it may help:** Route feasibility, timing, provider availability, pricing, and service scope are reviewed before a relocation request is confirmed.

**Why customers choose Waggies:** Unknown until supported by customer interviews or attributed feedback.

## Switching Dynamics

These are hypotheses until validated with customers.

**Push:** Unclear availability, fragmented coordination, or uncertainty about requirements may prompt an owner to seek help.

**Pull:** A local team that reviews the request and explains service scope and next steps may feel easier to work with.

**Habit:** Owners may continue using a familiar clinic, boarding provider, or self-managed travel process.

**Anxiety:** Owners may worry about care quality, travel compliance, cost, delays, and handing a pet to another person or provider.
