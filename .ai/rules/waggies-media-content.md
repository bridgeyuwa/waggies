# Waggies Media and Content

## Photography

Prefer authentic, high-quality pet-care imagery that supports trust and service context. Avoid generic low-quality stock, overly staged novelty images, and imagery that conflicts with the premium/warm Waggies character.

Do not redesign a section around a placeholder image whose final crop/aspect ratio is unknown.

## Media containers

For repeated service cards, testimonials, team/animal images, and galleries, use explicit aspect ratios and intentional `object-fit` behavior. Prevent layout shift by reserving image dimensions.

## Text over imagery

Text over a photo must remain readable across the actual responsive crop. Use a deliberate overlay/treatment or move text onto a surface if necessary. Do not rely on a single ideal crop.

## Icons

Use size-appropriate icons. Do not enlarge small UI glyphs into hero illustrations. When a small icon needs more visual presence, use a supporting shape/surface around it.

## Meaningful content

Do not place essential service names, prices, contact information, instructions, or actions only inside images. Keep critical content as real text/controls.

## Alt text

Meaningful images need concise contextual alt text. Decorative images should not create screen-reader noise.
